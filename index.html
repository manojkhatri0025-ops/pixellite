GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens in your browser on your device. PixelLite does not send your selected image to a server.

Can I reduce file size without losing quality?

Sometimes. Lossless optimization can make some files smaller without changing image data, but it usually cannot shrink every photo by a large amount. This tool uses browser image encoding, so reducing the size substantially can reduce some detail.

Why didn’t my image reach the target size?

The target is approximate. The format, dimensions and complexity of an image affect its final size. PixelLite keeps the dimensions unchanged and prioritizes a usable result over an unrealistic target.

What is the maximum image size?

You can select images up to 100 MB. Compression happens on your device, so very large or high-resolution images may need more memory than some phones or browsers have available.

OUR PURPOSE

About PixelLite

PixelLite is a lightweight image compression tool designed to help you make image files easier to store, share and use online. Choose an image, set an approximate target size and download the result.

Private by design

Image processing takes place in your browser. Your selected image is not uploaded to PixelLite’s server. No account is required, and your original dimensions are preserved.

Quality matters

Smaller files can require removing image data. We give you control over a target size and output format, but the best result depends on the image. Review the compressed image before using it.
